# What is the Yarvis Reality Graph?

The **Yarvis Reality Graph (YRG)** is the canonical representation of operational reality across identities, governance, relationships, context, knowledge, decisions, and execution.

Yarvis is an Operational Reality Platform. The YRG is its architectural core.

The YRG does not mirror a vendor system, synchronize a set of tables, or replace source applications. It connects evidence from those sources into a coherent, explainable representation of the operation.

Every domain enriches the YRG. No domain replaces it.

---

# The Layers of Operational Reality

```text
Reality
  ↓
Identity → Governance → Relationships → Operational Context
  ↓                                      ↓
Knowledge → Decision Intelligence → Execution
```

| Layer | Question | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Who exists? | People, organizations, canonical Parties |
| Governance | Who has authority? | Ownership, control, representation, responsibilities |
| Relationships | How are actors connected? | Memberships, commercial and operating relationships |
| Operational Context | Where does work occur? | Assets, projects, sites, opportunities, cases |
| Knowledge | What is known? | Documents, observations, evidence, conversations, events |
| Decision Intelligence | What should happen next? | Recommendations, risk detection, priorities, opportunity discovery |
| Execution | What actually happened? | Human actions, tasks, workflows, automation |

The layers are conceptual, not isolated systems. Each layer provides the context required for the next one to be interpreted faithfully.

---

# How Domains Extend the YRG

A business domain contributes evidence, relationships, context, or decisions to the shared graph. It must not create a competing representation of the same operational reality.

For example:

- Commercial domains enrich Parties, relationships, opportunities, and evidence.
- Asset domains enrich assets, sites, operating responsibilities, and lifecycle events.
- Operational Evidence Consolidation enriches knowledge through Documents, Observations, provenance, and confirmations.
- Store Intelligence enriches operational context and produces explainable recommendations for human decision.
- Mission Control navigates the YRG through the decisions that require attention.

Source systems remain observers. Their identifiers and data are evidence with provenance, not the definition of canonical reality.

---

# How Artificial Intelligence Reasons over the YRG

Artificial Intelligence reasons over structured, evidenced context in the YRG. It may recommend, detect conflicts, identify missing context, and assist execution under explicit authority.

It shall not invent operational reality, canonical identities, authority, ownership, or evidence.

Every recommendation must be explainable through its supporting evidence, provenance, reasoning, confidence, and expected impact.

Autonomy is progressive: Yarvis remembers, organizes, understands, recommends, coordinates, and only then may execute under explicit authority.

---

# How Mission Control Navigates the YRG

Mission Control is not a separate source of truth or a dashboard that derives its own reality.

It is the operational decision surface for the YRG. It should help a human answer:

> What is the best decision I can make right now?

Mission Control presents relevant decision intelligence with its safe, explainable operational context. It does not replace human accountability.

---

# Architectural Consequences

- Model reality before software.
- Preserve canonical identity before identifiers.
- Represent authority explicitly; never infer it.
- Preserve evidence, provenance, conflict, and historical continuity.
- Enrich the shared YRG instead of introducing disconnected domain silos.
- Treat AI as infrastructure for recommendation and assistance, never as a substitute for accountability.
- Design capabilities for better decision quality, speed, confidence, and explainability.
