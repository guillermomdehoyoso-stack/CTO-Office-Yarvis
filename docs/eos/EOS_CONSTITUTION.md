# EOS Constitution

## Status

**PROPOSED**

**NOT RATIFIED**

## Preamble

The Engineering Operating System (EOS) is a domain-independent governance
system for long-lived software engineering. It governs human and AI
collaboration through explicit authority, reproducible evidence, and
reviewable process.

Authority is created through governance.

Authority is not created through conversations.

Authority is not created through memory.

Authority is not created through tools.

## Article I - Authority Hierarchy

The constitutional authority hierarchy is:

1. Constitution
2. Ratified Governance
3. Ratified Architecture
4. Ratified ADRs
5. Ratified Roadmaps
6. Current State
7. Session Logs
8. Chat Conversations

When conflicts exist, the higher authority prevails.

## Article II - Evidence

Engineering decisions must be reproducible.

No engineering decision may exist solely because an AI asserted it.

Every engineering decision must have documentary evidence.

## Article III - Separation of Responsibilities

Engineering responsibilities are distinct and explicitly separated:

1. Proposal
2. Review
3. Ratification
4. Implementation
5. Validation

No single actor or process may perform all of these responsibilities for the
same engineering change.

## Article IV - Independent Review

Independent review is mandatory before ratification.

Independent review means:

- independent session;
- repository bootstrap;
- repository evidence; and
- no hidden conversational state.

## Article V - Historical Integrity

Engineering history is append-only.

Corrections produce new proposals.

History is never rewritten.

## Article VI - Deterministic Reconstruction

Independent agents reading the same repository should reconstruct equivalent
engineering state.

Ambiguity must generate Stop-and-Report behavior.

## Article VII - Reuse Before Build

Engineering preference order is:

1. Open Standards
2. Existing Mature Capabilities
3. Thin Integration
4. Extension
5. Custom Core

Equivalent open standards should be preferred over vendor-specific capability
whenever they satisfy engineering requirements.

Vendor-specific capability is justified only when it provides meaningful
differentiated value.

## Article VIII - Agent Boundaries

Agents may:

- analyze;
- review;
- propose;
- generate; and
- summarize.

Agents may not:

- ratify;
- approve their own output;
- change authority; or
- silently modify governance.

## Article IX - Human Authority

Ultimate engineering responsibility always belongs to an identified human
authority.

## Article X - Constitutional Evolution

The Constitution may evolve only through its own governance process.

## Article XI - Epistemic Humility

When evidence conflicts:

1. Stop.
2. Explain.
3. Request review.

Never fabricate certainty.

## Article XII - Domain Independence

EOS is domain-independent.

Domain knowledge shall exist in Domain Packs.

The Constitution governs all domains equally.
